None beyond the common frontmatter in `../CLAUDE.md`.

## Body

```
# <Topic>

One-paragraph summary (2–4 sentences).

## Key facts
- 3–6 concise bullets.

## Sources

## Go deeper
```

## Length

≤ 300 words. No filler, no disclaimers.

## Source quality

Encyclopaedic (Wikipedia, Britannica), academic abstracts, reputable journalism. No SEO farms.
